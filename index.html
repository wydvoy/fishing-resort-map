Rarity
Zone boxes, numbers and fish counts come from Fish/setSightArea.bin. Species and weights come from each area's fishFree table. The map is rendered from the stage models, so small details may be rough.
Guide

How to read this map

Each area is drawn from above, with the game x axis to the right and z axis up. Water is blue (darker is deeper), land is green, rock is grey, sand is beige, piers and buildings are brown, and tree cover is a darker green tint.

  • 01Zones are the coloured boxes. Fish of a zone only spawn inside its box.
  • 02Zone numbers match the SPOT numbers in the in-game debug overlay.
  • 03Time bands: Morning 06:00 to 11:59, Day 12:00 to 15:59, Evening 16:00 to 17:59, Night 18:00 to 05:59.
  • 04Fish count is how many fish the zone holds in a band.
  • 05Share is the chance a fish spawned in the zone is that species: its weight divided by the zone's total weight in that band. Weight 0 means it does not spawn there in that band.
  • 06Search a fish to light up every zone where it can spawn in the chosen band, with its share.
