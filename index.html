How to read this

Each area is drawn from above, with the game x axis to the right and z axis up. Water is blue (darker is deeper), land is green, rock is grey, sand is beige, piers and buildings are brown, and tree cover is a darker green tint.

  • Zones are the coloured boxes. Fish of a zone only spawn inside its box.
  • Zone numbers are the same as the SPOT numbers in the in-game debug overlay.
  • Time bands: the game uses four bands. Morning 06:00 to 11:59, Day 12:00 to 15:59, Evening 16:00 to 17:59, Night 18:00 to 05:59.
  • Fish count is how many fish the zone holds in a band.
  • Share is the chance that a fish spawned in the zone is that species: its weight divided by the sum of all weights of the zone in that band. A species with weight 0 does not spawn there in that band.
  • Search a species to light up every zone where it can spawn in the chosen band, with its share.

Rarity

Zone boxes, numbers and fish counts come from Fish/setSightArea.bin. Species and weights come from the fishFree tables of each area. The map image is rendered from the stage models, so small details may be rough.
